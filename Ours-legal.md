# Privacy Policy

**Ours — Shared Budget**
_Last updated: September 15, 2026_

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Ours app ("the App," "we," "us") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using the App, you agree to this policy.

---

## Summary

- The App works **locally on your device** by default. You can create and track budgets without an account.
- If you **sign in** (via Sign in with Apple) and use **shared budgets** or **cloud backup**, your budget data is stored on our backend so it can sync across devices and with people you invite.
- We do **not** sell your data, show third-party ads, or connect to your bank.
- You can delete your data at any time.

---

## Information We Collect

### Information you provide

- **Account identity.** When you sign in with Sign in with Apple, we receive a unique Apple user identifier and, if you choose to share it, your name. We do **not** receive your Apple ID password. If Apple provides a relay email, we may receive that relay address; we do not receive your real email unless you choose to share it.
- **Display name.** A name you set to identify yourself to other members of a shared budget.
- **Budget content.** Budgets, categories, and transactions you create — including amounts, dates, merchant names, and descriptions you enter.

### Information created through use

- **Membership and sharing data.** When you host or join a shared budget, we store which budgets you belong to and your role, so the right people can access the right budget.
- **Authorship.** Transactions record which member created them, so shared budgets can show who added what.

### Information we do **not** collect

- We do **not** connect to, access, or import from your bank or financial institutions.
- We do **not** collect your contacts, location, photos, or device identifiers for tracking.
- We do **not** use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs that track you across apps.

---

## How We Use Your Information

We use your information only to provide the App's features:

- To let you create and track budgets on your device.
- To sync your budgets across your devices and with members you invite (when signed in and using shared or backed-up budgets).
- To show member names and authorship within a shared budget.
- To operate the paid subscription (see "Subscriptions" below).

We do **not** use your information for advertising, and we do **not** sell or rent it to anyone.

---

## How Your Information Is Shared

- **With members you invite.** Content in a shared budget is visible to the people you invite to that budget (and to people who join with a code you share). Only invited members can see a shared budget — it is not public and is not discoverable by anyone else.
- **Service providers.** We use the following providers to run the App:
  - **Supabase** — backend database and authentication that stores your synced budget data.
  - **Apple** — Sign in with Apple, and App Store subscription processing.
- We do **not** share your information with advertisers or data brokers.

---

## Data Storage and Security

- Data you keep only on your device stays on your device.
- Synced and shared data is stored on our backend (Supabase) and transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS).
- Access to shared budgets is restricted so that only members of a given budget can read or write its data.
- No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

---

## Subscriptions

Some features (hosting shared budgets and cloud backup) require a paid subscription, offered as monthly or yearly plans with a free trial where indicated. Payment is processed by **Apple** through your App Store account; we do not receive or store your payment card details. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage or cancel your subscription in your device's App Store account settings.

---

## Your Choices and Rights

- **Use without an account.** You can use core budgeting features locally without signing in.
- **Access and deletion.** You can delete individual budgets and transactions within the App. You can also permanently delete your entire account and all associated backend data at any time from Profile → Delete Account. You may also contact us at the email below.
- **Leaving shared budgets.** You can leave a shared budget at any time, which removes your access to it.
- Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights (such as access, correction, or deletion) under laws like GDPR or CCPA. Contact us to exercise these rights.

---

## Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

---

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted at this URL with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
